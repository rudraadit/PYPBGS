Pragya Yuva Prakosth (PYP) Begusarai

Founded in 2018 by a small group of inspired youth volunteers, Pragya Yuva Prakosth (PYP) Begusarai has grown into a premier non-profit youth collective in the Begusarai region of Bihar.

As the official youth wing of All World Gayatri Pariwar (AWGP), PYP Begusarai operates directly under the spiritual and philosophical guidelines of Pandit Shriram Sharma Acharya. The organization is committed to channelizing youth potential toward character refinement, eradicating social evils, and nation building.

Community Sanctuary

Centre of Youth Awakening & Sadhana

Operating from the Gayatri Shaktipeeth in Sarvodaya Nagar, youth from schools, colleges, and professional sectors gather regularly for collective introspection, sanskar training, and grassroots community service.

A Sage, a Visionary and a Reformer

“A sage, a visionary and a reformer, Shri Ram Sharma Acharya initiated Yug Nirman Yojna — a movement for mass transformation. He lived a disciplined life of devout austerity, visited Himalayas several times and attained spiritual eminence.”

It is time that we awaken, change our attitude and expand our sensitivities to the changing times by transforming our thoughts. Thought provoking — life transforming literature has been the basis of this mission initiated for transformation of era.

Author of over 3,200 books illuminating every aspect of human life, science, and spirituality, his teachings continue to guide millions of youth across the globe to lead lives of purpose, purity, and selfless service.
